## Why Packbound exists

A hunter has a few things to keep in order: a pet that is out, alive and fed, arrows or bullets in the ammo slot, and an aspect that is up. Forgetting one of them costs damage, or a walk back to town.

Packbound shows a card when one of these needs you. Click the card and it fixes it: it calls, revives or feeds your pet, casts your aspect, or sets a waypoint to the nearest ammo vendor. It only loads on hunters.

## What it does

- **Your pet.** A card when your pet isn't out, has died or is hungry. Click to call, revive or feed it. [More](features/pet.html)
- **Ammo and the nearest ammo vendor.** A card when your arrows or bullets run low. It names the closest vendor who sells your kind of ammo; click for a waypoint. [More](features/ammo.html)
- **Aspects and Trueshot Aura.** A card when no aspect is up, and a timer for Trueshot Aura once you have the talent. [More](features/aspects.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: beside the game menu you get Aspect of the Cheetah, Aspect of the Pack, the nearest ammo vendor and your Hearthstone. [More](features/travel-panel.html)
- **Buff key.** One key that casts what the top card asks for. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations, skins and your layout.** Tooltips that say what a card is for, three looks, and frames you can move and resize. [More](features/)

## The Bound family

Packbound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core (LibBound): Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest) and Felbound (warlock). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
