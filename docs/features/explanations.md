# Explanations in tooltips

Hover over a Packbound card, or a row on the travel panel, and the tooltip says what it is for and why it matters. Made for players new to the hunter.

## What it does

Some of the explanations:

| Where | The tooltip says |
| --- | --- |
| Call your pet | Your pet fights with you and takes hits for you. Call it whenever it isn't out. |
| Your pet is dead | Your pet has died. Revive Pet brings it back (it costs mana and takes a few seconds). |
| Your pet is hungry | A hungry pet does less damage and can even run away. Feeding it food it likes makes it happy again. |
| No food | Your pet eats only some kinds of food (meat, fish, bread, fruit...). Keep some in your bags. |
| Ammo | Your bow or gun needs arrows or bullets in the ammo slot. Buy more from a general goods or weapon vendor. |
| No aspect | An aspect is a stance for hunters: Aspect of the Hawk gives ranged attack power, Monkey dodge. |
| Trueshot Aura | Trueshot Aura gives you and your group more attack power. |
| Aspect of the Cheetah | Aspect of the Cheetah makes you run faster, but you are dazed when something hits you. For travel only. |
| Hearthstone | Your Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to. Talk to an innkeeper to change it. |

A card you can click to cast also says "Click to cast (out of combat)".

## How to use it

Hover over a card or a row. To turn the explanations off, untick **Explain things in tooltips**, or type `/pack explain`.

## Settings

In `/pack`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Plain-word help when you hover over a card or button. Turn it off once you know it all. |

## Good to know

**With explanations off,** a card you can click to cast and the travel panel rows still show a tooltip with the name and how to use it (for example "Click to cast."). Cards with nothing to cast show no tooltip then.

**Cards don't block clicks on the world.** A card that only explains reacts to the mouse for its tooltip but lets clicks through. Cards you can click take the click.

**While the frames are unlocked,** cards show no explanation: the mouse is then for dragging.
