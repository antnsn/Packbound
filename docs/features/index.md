# Features

Packbound does a few small hunter jobs for you. Each page below covers one of them: what it does, how to use it, the settings in the `/pack` window, and the things that surprise people. New to the addon? Start with [your pet](pet.html) and [ammo](ammo.html).

- [Your pet](pet.html): a card to call your pet, revive it or feed it.
- [Ammo and the nearest ammo vendor](ammo.html): a card when arrows or bullets run low, with a waypoint to the closest vendor.
- [Aspects and Trueshot Aura](aspects.html): a card when no aspect is up, and a timer for Trueshot Aura.
- [Travel panel](travel-panel.html): Aspect of the Cheetah, Aspect of the Pack, the nearest ammo vendor and your Hearthstone beside the Escape menu.
- [Buff key](buff-key.html): one key that casts what the top card asks for.
- [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html): a warning before they run out.
- [Explanations in tooltips](explanations.html): what a card or button is for, when you hover over it.
- [Frames and the minimap button](frames.html): moving and resizing every frame, the grid, and the minimap button.
- [Skins](skins.html): Wild, classic Blizzard or flat, to match the rest of your UI.
