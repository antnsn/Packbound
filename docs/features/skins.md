# Skins

Packbound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Wild** (default) | Dark leather, a warm amber edge and claw marks in the corners. A faint hunter crest behind each card, and a slow amber rim while a card needs you. | The default UI, or if you just like the hunter look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own gray and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons, white text. | A mostly default UI where amber would stand out. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark gray, Arial Narrow text. Amber stays as the one accent. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

1. Type `/pack` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Packbound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

Colors that carry meaning stay the same in every skin: red for anything missing or running low.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Packbound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.

**Does Flat take its colors from EllesmereUI or ElvUI?**
No. Flat uses its own dark gray and Packbound's amber.
