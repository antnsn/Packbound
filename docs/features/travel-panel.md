# Travel panel

Press Escape, and beside the game menu Packbound shows a small **Travel** panel with the things you use to get somewhere.

## What it does

![The travel panel beside the game menu]({{ '/screenshots/travel.png' | relative_url }})

The panel has these rows, top to bottom:

| Row | A click |
| --- | --- |
| **Aspect of the Cheetah** | Casts it. You run faster, but you are dazed when something hits you. |
| **Aspect of the Pack** | Casts it. Your whole group runs faster, with the same daze. |
| **Ammo:** the nearest ammo vendor, with the zone and how many yards away | Sets a waypoint to that vendor, like a click on the [ammo card](ammo.html). |
| **Hearthstone:** the inn it is set to | Uses your Hearthstone. |

- An aspect you haven't learned yet is grayed out, with the level you learn it at: Cheetah at level 20, Pack at level 40, both from a hunter trainer.
- The ammo row is always there, also when your ammo is fine. When Packbound can't tell where you are (inside a dungeon) or knows no vendor on this continent, the row says so and does nothing.
- The Hearthstone row only shows when you have a Hearthstone in your bags.
- After a click the game menu closes.

## How to use it

1. Press Escape.
2. Click a row.

## Settings

![The Travel page]({{ '/screenshots/options-travel.png' | relative_url }})

In `/pack`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Shows or hides the panel. |

## Good to know

**The panel doesn't show in combat.**
It hides when a fight starts and only shows with the game menu out of combat. The game doesn't let addons cast travel spells in combat anyway.

**Hover over a row** for what it does, and for a spell you don't have yet, where to learn it.

**The panel can't be moved.** It always sits to the right of the game menu.
